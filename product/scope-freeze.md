# MVP Scope Freeze

**Frozen on:** ________________
**Unfreezes on:** ________________ *(first paying customer, or 4 months — whichever is first)*

---

## The question this MVP answers

> Will a 400-member Iranian gym abandon paid desktop software for GymOS, and pay
> monthly for it?

Nothing that doesn't serve that question is in scope.

---

## In — 13 items

1. Members & leads, one lifecycle, one table
2. Plans: duration · session-count · hybrid. Freeze. Renewal
3. Tuition, arrears ledger, partial payment
4. Member wallet
5. Check-in: QR + card + manual, **offline tolerant**
6. Lockers: public + VIP
7. Buffet POS on wallet
8. SMS engine: idempotency, quiet hours, credit accounting
9. Automation engine + **6 recipes only**
10. Retention Intelligence, rules-based, with stated reasons
11. Owner mobile dashboard, 5 tiles
12. Trainer commission settlement
13. Migration: Excel/CSV + **one** incumbent format

Plus, non-negotiably: Solar Hijri throughout · Persian/RTL · Toman display over
Rial storage · drawer close with variance · audit log on money.

---

## Out — say no to all of these

Direct debit · member PWA · lead desk · booking & classes · workout & nutrition
programs · body measurements · AI anything · multi-branch · hardware bridge ·
corporate accounts · API & webhooks · visual workflow builder · website ·
reviews · dynamic pricing · accounting · payroll · native app · marketplace ·
benchmarking · forecasting · a seventh automation recipe

---

## Rules

1. **Nothing is added to the In list before the unfreeze date.** Not by a
   prospect, not by a design partner, not by me.
2. A request that arrives twice from two different gyms goes on a list. The list
   is read at unfreeze, not before.
3. The only permitted change is **removal** — if something proves unnecessary,
   cut it and note why.
4. If a deal genuinely depends on an out-of-scope item, that is a decision to
   take deliberately, in writing, with the date and what it displaced. Not a
   quiet addition.

## Why this exists

You are one person. The strategy report identifies roadmap sprawl back toward the
full platform vision as the **most likely failure mode, and the one entirely
within your control**.

The original brief listed roughly 90 features across four layers. This list has
13. That gap is the product decision.
