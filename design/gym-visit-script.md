# Gym Visit Script — 10 visits, week 1

One page. Print it. Fill one per gym. Target **10 gyms, 300–1,500 members, with
a receptionist and a gate/fingerprint reader**, in Tehran or Karaj.

You are not selling. You are collecting six answers that unblock the build and
one number that tells you whether this gym will ever buy.

## The qualifying question — ask this second

> **«از شهریه ماه گذشته، چقدرش هنوز وصول نشده؟»**
> *(Of last month's tuition, how much is still uncollected?)*

| Response | Reading |
|---|---|
| Gives a number, and winces | **Best case.** They feel the pain and measure it. They will buy. |
| Gives a rough number | Good. Quantify it with them. |
| "I don't know" | They aren't ready. Still finish the visit — the field data is useful. |
| "We don't have that problem" | Probe once: do members ever train while owing? If yes, they have it and don't track it. |

**Record the number.** Across ten gyms this is your market-sizing evidence and
the baseline your hold-out measures against.

## Six questions that unblock the build

**1 — Shared phone numbers** ← *the only one that can change the schema*
> «آیا پیش می‌آید چند نفر از یک خانواده با یک شماره موبایل ثبت‌نام کنند؟»
*(Do several family members ever register on one mobile number?)*

Yes ☐ No ☐ Sometimes ☐ — **If two of ten say yes, stop and tell me before writing migrations.**

**2 — Session accounting**
> «اشتراک ۱۲ جلسه‌ای اگر تا آخر ماه استفاده نشود، جلسات می‌سوزد یا منتقل می‌شود؟»
*(On a 12-session membership, do unused sessions expire at month end or carry over?)*

Expire ☐ Carry over ☐ Depends on the plan ☐ We don't sell session plans ☐

**3 — Gendered hours**
> «ساعات آقایان و بانوان چطور تقسیم می‌شود؟ همزمان در دو سالن، یا در ساعات جدا؟»
*(How are men's and women's hours split — two halls at once, or separate hours?)*

Separate hours, whole gym ☐ Concurrent, separate halls ☐ Single gender ☐

**4 — Trainer commission**
> «پورسانت مربی روی شهریه‌ی فروخته‌شده حساب می‌شود یا شهریه‌ی وصول‌شده؟»
*(Is trainer commission on tuition sold, or tuition actually collected?)*

Sold ☐ Collected ☐ Fixed salary ☐ Mixed ☐

**5 — How they chase money today**
> «الان بدهی‌ها را چطور پیگیری می‌کنید؟»
*(How do you follow up on debts right now?)*

Phone calls ☐ SMS, manual ☐ In person at the door ☐ Not at all ☐

Who does it, and how long does it take them per week? ______

**6 — Automation consent** ← *this one shapes the pitch, not the code*
> «اگر سیستم خودکار به اسم باشگاه به اعضا پیامک بدهد، راحت هستید؟ یا ترجیح می‌دهید اول تأیید کنید؟»
*(If the system automatically SMSes members in the gym's name, are you comfortable — or would you want to approve first?)*

Fully automatic ☐ Approve each batch ☐ Not comfortable ☐

**If most say "approve each batch," the automation pitch weakens and the
messaging becomes assisted rather than autonomous. Better to learn it now.**

## Before you leave

- ☐ Photographed the door controller and its label
- ☐ Asked whether they'd give you a **copy of their data file** for a migration test — offer to do a free import demo in return. *This is how you unblock the extractor spike without buying software.*
- ☐ Noted the receptionist's name. They are your champion, not the owner.
- ☐ Asked who installed the gate, and got the installer's number. **That is your channel.**
