# GymOS — Sales Kit

You are the only salesperson. This is what you say, in what order, and what you
must never lead with.

---

## 0. Correction: do not lead with total cost of ownership

The strategy report told you to publish a 3-year TCO comparison. **I ran the
numbers and that advice was wrong.** Over three years, for a 600-member gym:

| Option | 3-year cost (Toman) | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| My-Gym | **~8–20M** | 5.5M one-time + SMS top-ups (+ unknown upgrade/support renewals) |
| Parsian | ~20–60M? | Modular phases + support. **Unpublished — fill from your visits** |
| Gymona Gold | **74.7M** | 24.9M/yr annual, published |
| **GymOS Growth** | **~104M** | 34M/yr annual + ~2M SMS |

GymOS is the **most expensive option on the sheet.** A TCO argument loses, and
publishing one hands the incumbent the win. Cut it.

*(SMS estimate assumes ~2,300 Persian segments/month at shared-line rates —
verify against your actual panel pricing before quoting.)*

### What you lead with instead

Not cost. **Exposure.**

> **«شما الان حدود ۲۲۵ میلیون تومان شهریه‌ی معوق روی دستتان است. ما بخشی از آن را وصول می‌کنیم.»**

For a 600-member gym at 2.5M average tuition: gross monthly dues ~1,500M Toman.
If 15% runs late — and your visit data will tell you the real figure — that's
**~225M Toman of exposure every month.**

Recover even a fifth of it and that's 45M Toman a month, against a 3.4M bill.
**That ratio is the entire sale.** Price stops being the conversation.

This is also exactly why the 10% hold-out in `automation-recipes.md` §0 is
non-negotiable: without it the recovery figure is a claim. With it, it's theirs.

## 1. The move that wins deals: migrate before you demo

**Do not give a generic demo. Ever.**

1. At the first visit, ask for a copy of their data file — offer a free import demo in exchange.
2. Import it. Fix whatever breaks. Time yourself.
3. Come back and demo **their gym, their members, their arrears.**

The moment the owner sees their own delinquent member list on your screen, with
a real Toman total they've never seen aggregated before, the conversation changes
from "what does this software do" to "how soon can we start."

It also proves the 48-hour switch claim instead of asserting it — and it makes
the migration spike pay for itself as a sales asset.

## 2. Demo script — 7 minutes

Keep it to seven. They have a gym to run.

**Minute 1 — their arrears list.** Open on it. Don't introduce the product.
> «این لیست اعضای بدهکار شماست. جمعش ۲۲۳ میلیون تومان است. این عدد را قبلاً جایی دیده بودید؟»

Almost always: no. Let the silence sit.

**Minute 2 — how it gets chased.** Show one automation. Show the queued Persian
SMS with the real amount in it. Show the aging buckets.
> «هر هفته خودش پیگیری می‌کند. کسی لازم نیست تلفن بزند.»

**Minute 3 — the unplugged cable.** Physically unplug the network. Check a member
in. Show the amber queue counter. Plug it back in. Show it sync.
> «اینترنت باشگاه قطع شود، پذیرش کار می‌کند.»

This is the single most memorable thirty seconds of the demo and it kills the
biggest objection to cloud software before they raise it.

**Minute 4 — the owner's phone.** Hand them your phone with their gym on it.
Revenue, attendance, arrears recovered. At night, from home.
> «این را از خانه می‌بینید. نرم‌افزار فعلی‌تان فقط روی کامپیوتر پذیرش است.»

**Minute 5 — their hardware still works.** Show the gate/fingerprint reader
syncing, or if the bridge isn't built yet, say plainly: *"ما با گیت فعلی شما کار
می‌کنیم"* only once it's true. **Never promise this before the spike confirms it.**

**Minute 6 — reception speed.** Have the receptionist check three people in.
Two seconds each, no mouse. They'll notice. The receptionist is your champion.

**Minute 7 — the offer.**
> «مهاجرت رایگان، ۴۸ ساعت. سی روز استفاده کامل، بدون پرداخت. اگر نپسندیدید، داده‌هایتان را کامل به شما برمی‌گردانیم.»

Stop. Don't add features. Don't mention AI.

## 3. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **«۵.۵ میلیون یک‌بار دادم، چرا ماهانه بدهم؟»** | Don't defend the price. Reframe: «آن ۵.۵ میلیون بدهی‌های شما را وصول نمی‌کند. ماهی ۳.۴ میلیون می‌دهید که ۴۵ میلیون برگردانیم.» Then offer the annual plan so it reads as one payment like the licence they know. |
| **«اطلاعاتم روی اینترنت باشد؟»** | Three structural answers, not reassurance: hosted in Iran, works offline (you just showed them), and one-click full export any time. «هر لحظه بخواهید، تمام داده‌ها را می‌گیرید و می‌روید.» |
| **«اینترنت قطع شود چه؟»** | Already answered in minute 3. If they ask anyway, unplug it again. |
| **«پشتیبانی پارسیان تلفن را جواب می‌دهد و می‌آید باشگاه.»** | Don't compete — match it. «شماره من همین است. نصب و آموزش حضوری. اِنی‌دسک.» Then, if the installer is in the room, that relationship is your ally, not your obstacle. |
| **«جیمونا ارزان‌تر است.»** | Honest and specific: «بله، ارزان‌تر است. جیمونا بدهی‌های شما را پیگیری نمی‌کند و با گیت شما کار نمی‌کند.» Never claim Gymona is bad — it isn't. Claim it does something different. |
| **«الان وقت ندارم / بعد از عید.»** | «مهاجرت را همین حالا رایگان انجام می‌دهم، سی روز استفاده کنید. اگر نپسندیدید هیچ هزینه‌ای ندارید.» The free trial exists to defeat exactly this. |
| **«کارمزد پرداخت مستقیم زیاد نیست؟»** | Pre-empt it, don't wait: ~7,000 Toman per collection. «برای وصول ۲.۵ میلیون، هفت هزار تومان. در مقابل شهریه‌ای که اصلاً وصول نمی‌شود.» |
| **«اپلیکیشن اختصاصی دارید؟»** | «پورتال موبایل عضو دارید، بدون نیاز به نصب.» Don't promise a native app. It's V2 and only for a chain that pays for it. |

## 4. Qualification — walk away fast

Ask the uncollected-tuition question in the first five minutes, every time.

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Knows the number, winces | **Close them.** Book the migration on the spot. |
| Rough number, cares | Strong. Get the data file. |
| "I don't know" | Not ready. Be friendly, get the installer's contact, leave. |
| "We don't have that problem" | One probe: do members ever train while owing? If yes they have it. If they still say no, leave. |
| Under 250 members | Out of ICP. Note them for V2 self-serve. |
| No receptionist | Out of ICP. Nobody will operate it. |

Your scarcest resource is your own time. Ten qualified conversations beat fifty
polite ones.

## 5. Pricing conversation

- **Lead with annual.** 34M once reads like a licence; 3.4M monthly reads like a subscription they'll resent. Same money, different frame. Target 50%+ of new business on annual — it also fixes your payback period.
- **Never discount the first customer below your published price.** Discount the *duration* instead (two months free) or throw in SMS credit. A discounted price becomes the market's reference forever.
- **Disclose the annual indexation clause up front**, in writing, on the pricing page. Iranian buyers accept it because every supplier does it — but only if it wasn't sprung on them.
- **Design partners are free for 6 months, explicitly and in writing**, in exchange for weekly access and a reference. Not a discount — a different agreement. Keep the two categories clean.

## 7. What not to say

- **Never say "AI."** Your buyer does not want it, and the research says Iranian fitness operators rank technology trends low. Say «خودکار».
- **Never demo a feature you haven't built.** Especially the hardware bridge.
- **Never quote a recovery number you haven't measured** for that gym or a comparable one. Say «معمولاً» and give the range, or say you'll measure it in their first month.
- **Never disparage Gymona or Parsian.** Owners talk to each other. Differentiate, don't attack.
